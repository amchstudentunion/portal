Dispatch #01 • August 2026 • Union Editorial

Why We Must Operate Under the Cloak of Anonymity

"True representation cannot exist when individual students bear the personal administrative fallout of speaking unfiltered truth."

In a medical institution—particularly within the rigorous hierarchies of a tier-3 medical setup—authority is absolute. From clinical posting assessments and viva marks to hostel roll-calls, a student’s academic progression is perpetually subject to institutional goodwill.

The Burden of Individual Identity

When an individual student raises questions regarding erratic mess charges, non-functional hostel water coolers, unreasonable gate curfews, or clinical duty rotations, that student ceases to be an advocate. They become a target for friction.

Anonymity is not a veil for cowardice; it is a shield for collective objectivity. By removing personal names, faces, and batch identities from the management of this platform, we eliminate the leverage of individual retribution.

A Collective Utility, Not a Personal Brand

This portal was not built to celebrate individual student leaders or serve as a resume builder for union elections. It exists as an infrastructure:

  • To provide unvarnished, practical orientation to freshers stepping into medical life.
  • To preserve institutional memory across batches (from Trigarianz to Zenarianz).
  • To offer an un-monitored, secure feedback channel for genuine student grievances.

As long as the voice belongs to the entire student collective, the identity of the host remains irrelevant. The portal will continue to run quietly in the background.
