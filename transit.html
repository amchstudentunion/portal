Suburban Campus Transit Guide

How to navigate to and from Arunai Medical College (Velu Nagar NH Bypass) to town centers, bus stands, and railway links.

1. Auto Fares & Shared Stands

Because the campus sits on the outer bypass, autos often quote inflated single-hire rates. Standard student norms:

Route Norm (Shared) Private Hire
Campus Gate ↔ Old Bus Stand ₹20 – ₹30 ₹120 – ₹150
Campus Gate ↔ Central Bus Stand ₹25 – ₹35 ₹140 – ₹180
Campus ↔ Railway Station — ₹150 – ₹200

2. Local Town Bus Stops

Frequent government town buses run past the Velu Nagar bypass stop during daylight hours:

  • Bypass Stage Buses: Board buses heading towards Chengam / Bangalore highway or Villupuram link.
  • Last Bus Advisory: Local town frequencies drop sharply after 08:30 PM. Always have a trusted auto driver number saved if returning from town near curfew hours[cite: 1].

3. Going Home (Weekend Transit)

Connecting to major hubs across Tamil Nadu from Central Bus Stand:

  • Chennai (Kilambakkam KCBT / CMBT): Direct SETC/TNSTC buses leave every 15–20 minutes via Tindivanam.
  • Vellore / Katpadi Jn: Regular direct buses; convenient for north-bound train connections.
  • Salem / Coimbatore / Trichy: Regular departures via Harur/Villupuram routes.
