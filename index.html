How to Survive First Year First Year Survival Kit 💀

Freshers' Academic & Practical Briefing “Padikkala na hall ticket thara maataanga”

1. Anatomy & Dissection Hall

Dress Code: Clean apron, ID card, hair tied back. Mandatory dissection kit with B.P. blade holder (#24), forceps, and gloves.

Read Cunningham's Dissection Manual before entering the hall. Respect cadaveric donors at all times.

1. DH Protocols (Anatomy)

“Apron illama DH ulla kaal vecha veliya thurathiruvaanga.”

BP blade #24 set, tooth forceps, and Cunningham textbook are non-negotiable. Don't touch bones without senior notes first.

2. Physiology Practical Decorum

Clinical Focus: Hematology experiments (RBC/WBC count, ESR, blood grouping) and human system tracings.

Keep logbooks and practical observation manuals signed weekly by tutors to avoid end-posting halts.

2. Physio Logbooks

“Last minute-la sign vanga mudiyadhu da!”

Get weekly RBC/WBC practical manual calculations signed on time. If tutors reject your observation chart, prepare for double assignments.

3. Biochemistry Cycles & Charts

Exam Strategy: Master metabolism pathways, enzyme kinetics cycles, and clinical case interpretations (LFT, RFT, lipid profiles).

Form small study clusters early on for active recall of chemical cycles.

3. Biochem Cycles

“Cycles mattum ozhunga draw panna pass aagirlam.”

Glycolysis, Krebs cycle, and Urea cycle charts must be memorized with cofactors. Draw them daily on rough paper.
