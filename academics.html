The Academic Survival Manual

Phase-by-phase survival roadmaps, textbook choices, and exam blueprints passed down across batches.

Anatomy: Dissection & Histology

  • Textbooks: B.D. Chaurasia (standard) or Vishram Singh (clinical flow). For Neuroanatomy, rely on Vishram Singh.
  • Dissection Hall: Never walk in without reading Cunningham's manual steps. Keep a dedicated bone set study group.
  • Histology: Draw diagrams with Sharpie/Pencil sets the same week practicals end to avoid pre-exam record backlogs.

Physiology: Concepts & Clinicals

  • Textbooks: Guyton & Hall for deep concepts; A.K. Jain or G.K. Pal for university exam-oriented answers.
  • Hematology Lab: Master RBC/WBC counts and calculation formulas early; examiners prioritize calculation neatness.

Biochemistry: Cycles & Nutrition

  • Textbooks: U. Satyanarayana for rapid revision; DM Vasudevan for clinical case integrations.
  • High-Yield: Glycolysis, TCA, HMP Shunt, Urea cycle, Lipid transport pathways, and Vitamin deficiency syndromes.

Pathology: General & Systemic

  • Textbooks: Robbins Basic Pathology (gold standard for concepts); Harsh Mohan or Ramadas Nayak for exam writing.
  • Practical Strategy: Learn to identify gross specimens by shape, capsule, cut surface, and necrosis patterns before microscopy.

Pharmacology: Drug Mechanisms & Tox

  • Textbooks: K.D. Tripathi (standard); Shanbhag for quick revisions and drug classifications.
  • Prescription Writing: Practice legible, standard format prescriptions with doses, frequency, and instructions.

Microbiology: Bugs & Immunity

  • Textbooks: Apurba Sastry (essential for systemic bacteriology and clinical parasitology).
  • Culture Media: Focus on MacConkey, Blood Agar, and selective media characteristics for viva.

Community Medicine (PSM)

  • Textbooks: Park’s Textbook of Preventive and Social Medicine.
  • Field Clinics: Take family study cases and epidemiological survey postings seriously for practical viva.

Ophthalmology & ENT

  • Ophtha: A.K. Khurana (corneal ulcers, cataracts, and glaucoma diagnosis).
  • ENT: P.L. Dhingra (CSOM, DNS, tracheostomy protocols, and audiometry interpretations).

Forensic Medicine & Toxicology (FMT)

  • Textbooks: K.S. Narayan Reddy / Gautam Biswas.
  • Key Topics: IPC codes, post-mortem changes, ballistics, and medical jurisprudence.

General Medicine

  • Textbooks: Davidson's Principles & Practice of Medicine; Mathews / Kundu for clinical bedside examination.
  • Ward Habit: Examine at least one respiratory and CVS patient daily during morning clinics.

General Surgery

  • Textbooks: Bailey & Love (standard); S. Das for bedside surgical elicitation and palpation signs.

OBG & Paediatrics

  • OBG: DC Dutta (Obstetrics) & Shaw’s (Gynaecology).
  • Paeds: OP Ghai (milestones, immunization schedules, and neonatal resuscitation).
